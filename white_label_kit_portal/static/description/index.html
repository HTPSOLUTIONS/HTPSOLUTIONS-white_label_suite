Your company look on the customer portal

A companion of White Label Kit: make your Odoo look like your company.

  • The “Powered by” line and the Odoo logo at the bottom of the portal documents (quotations, sales orders, invoices, tasks…) are hidden, or replaced by “Powered by your brand”.

Requires White Label Kit (installed automatically). Nothing changes until “Apply my branding” is switched on in Settings > White Label; switching it off brings back the standard look.

Enable it from the White Label dashboard (Applications tab). It never installs the application itself.

Odoo 17, 18, 19 and 20 · Odoo's copyright notice and the Enterprise subscription checks are left untouched.
